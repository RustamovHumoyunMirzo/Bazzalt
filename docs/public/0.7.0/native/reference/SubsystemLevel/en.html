SubsystemLevel

SubsystemLevel declares the values and operations listed below. Types, defaults and overloads are part of its contract; it is not an editor panel.

Startup levels

ValueLoad boundary and service readiness
Boot (0)Before OnBoot; no scene or drivers.
Window (1)Before OnBeforeWindowCreate; no native window yet.
Initialization (2)Before OnInit begins driver initialization, not after it finishes.
Content (3)Before OnLoad; core drivers are initialized, initial scene is not ready.
Ready (4)Before OnPostWindowCreate; scene and rendering surface are available.

A subsystem may listen to events at its own or higher level, never lower. Actual service readiness is checked separately: audio playback starts with OnPlay. Ready services cannot subscribe to Content-level OnSceneLoad; use OnSceneLoaded instead.
