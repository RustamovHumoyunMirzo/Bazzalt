VehicleDifferential

One driven axle or wheel pair. LeftWheel and RightWheel refer to VehicleWheel entity UUIDs within the same chassis. Ratio is final drive, Split distributes left/right torque, LimitedSlipRatio must exceed one, and TorqueShare is normalized across enabled differentials. A wheel may belong to only one differential.
