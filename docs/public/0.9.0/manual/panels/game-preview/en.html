Game preview controls

The Game viewport toolbar previews output without changing game build settings or saved camera components.

Camera selection

  • Not specified uses the normal output composition, including split-screen viewports and camera priorities.
  • Choose a camera by name to preview just its perspective across the panel. The camera's render target and authored viewport/projection are restored after rendering; selection is an editor override.
  • None shows a black panel, including no overlay GUI.

A disabled/deleted selected camera has no output. The list follows the active scene and returns to the default when its selected camera is removed. Switching projects resets the camera override.

Device size

Free Aspect follows the available panel dimensions. Fixed landscape, portrait and tablet resolutions render to a captured target at the chosen pixel dimensions and fit the image into the panel without stretching. Pointer coordinates are mapped back to those pixels for game GUI interaction. Fixed-size previews use readback and can cost more than native Free Aspect; they are a simulator, not a change to the final executable window or camera asset.
